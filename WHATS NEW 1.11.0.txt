WHISKER BAY 1.11.0 - FISH & BOSS ENCOUNTER UPDATE

FISHING & POPULATIONS
- Fish are less likely to ignore bait during ordinary hook descents.
- Shared fish populations are less crowded and gather into small schools
  spread across the water instead of lining up.
- Sonar labels avoid overlapping, and fish render in a clearer depth order.
- Improved distinct artwork and signature details for several fish species.

BOSSES
- Admin boss summons now appear as shared encounters on the selected
  player's island, with clear feedback for active bosses and cooldowns.
- Boss phase transitions use each boss's own colors and encounter messages.

MULTIPLAYER
- The multiplayer protocol remains version 8; use compatible protocol-8
  clients and servers.
