WHISKER BAY 1.6.0 - ATLANTIS UPDATE

Explore the Ruins of Atlantis, a new end-game island at 5,200 m. Reach
level 38, complete the Mariana Deep island quest, and bring the
Orichalcum Argo to sail there.

ATLANTIS AND RED HOLLOW
- Discover new Atlantis fish, rods, lures, gear, an enchantment, and
  the Kraken boss.
- Catch the Oracle Eel to reveal the Broken Trident quest.
- Recover the three shards, claim the Trident, and return to Poseidon's
  statue to open the Red Hollow portal.
- Defeat the Watcher Below and choose whether to repair or seal the gate.
- Keeper's Logs are collected while exploring and can be read in the
  Fishpedia.
- Discover the Lantern Grotto, Signal Vault, and Keeper's Archive.
  Their rare fish stay inside each cavern.
- First catches recover Keeper's Logs and the ruins blueprints; boss
  materials are consumed when crafting material-gated rods.

SHARED FISH
- Online players on the same island see the same server-selected Mythic.
- The first angler to hook it claims the fish. If it escapes, it returns;
  after a catch, the island's shared Mythic respawns after a cooldown.
- Boss hooks are also arbitrated by the server so only one angler can
  claim an island boss fight at a time.
- The Firstlight Koi is limited to three catches per server. Its remaining
  supply is saved between server restarts.
- All clients and the host server must use the same game build.

CONTROLS
- Z uses the equipped rod skill while the hook is underwater.
- Use the Statue button in Atlantis or Red Hollow to follow the portal
  quest and return between worlds.
