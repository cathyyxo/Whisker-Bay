WHISKER BAY 1.7.0 - THE BIG FISHING & POLISH UPDATE

FISHING
- Later islands are still challenging, but the reel bar, line tension and
  catch pacing have been rebalanced to feel fairer with stronger rods.
- Mythic and boss fights now draw from larger trick pools. You will face
  three to five different active tricks, and each trick has its own cooldown.
- Two new mutations can appear: Stormbound and Moonlit. Catch more of each
  mutation to unlock its upgrade bonuses.

NEW GEAR
- Coral Cat Collar: fish bite sooner.
- Moonbell Anklet: line tension builds more slowly.
- Keeper's Pearl Charm: improves luck and mutation odds.
- Accessory cards show their island unlock and equipped status.

ADMIN TOOLS
- Admins can summon a random regular Mythic for an island.
- Admins can spawn a limited Mythic, but only when server stock remains.
  The fish is shared with everyone on that island and a catch uses one
  server-wide copy, which remains tracked across restarts.
- Offline limited spawns are labelled as local test copies and do not use
  any server inventory.

QUALITY OF LIFE
- Surge is now F by default; Fishpedia is G. Existing custom bindings stay
  as they were, and the old default bindings migrate automatically.
- Update notes open once after an update and can always be revisited from
  Settings > What's New or the title screen.
- Multiplayer protocol is now version 7. The host server and all players
  need the same 1.7.0 build.
