WHISKER BAY 1.10.0 - THE PRESENTATION UPDATE

INTERFACE & READABILITY
- Refined the shared UI theme with clearer panel hierarchy, higher-contrast
  controls, responsive hover/pressed states, and consistent depth.
- Shop and inventory cards now have clearer selected/equipped treatments.
- Small-screen HUD buttons identify themselves on hover instead of relying
  on icon recognition alone.
- Fishing instructions now sit in a readable, adaptive hint panel.
- The title screen and menus have clearer visual grouping and spacing.
- Sliders, scrollbars, and text fields have stronger focus and interaction
  feedback.

GAMEPLAY
- Fishing rules and progression are unchanged in this presentation update.
- The multiplayer wire protocol is unchanged, so protocol-8 players and
  servers remain compatible with the 1.9.0 shared-fish release.
